# What do similar listing tools offer, and what do they charge?

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by:

## Question

What will a Seller compare this service to? Survey:

1. Crosslisting and listing-assistant tools (for example Vendoo, List Perfectly, Crosslist, and AI listing generators): what they do and their current prices.
2. AI listing features built into the platforms for free (Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Mercari, OfferUp).
3. Which of these do what the agent does: a recall check, price comps, buyer reply drafts, removing photo location data.

The goal is to find what nobody does for free, and what a casual Seller already pays for. Use the vendors' own pages for features and prices.
