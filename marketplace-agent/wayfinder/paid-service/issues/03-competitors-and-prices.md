# What do similar listing tools offer, and what do they charge?

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

What will a Seller compare this service to? Survey:

1. Crosslisting and listing-assistant tools (for example Vendoo, List Perfectly, Crosslist, and AI listing generators): what they do and their current prices.
2. AI listing features built into the platforms for free (Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Mercari, OfferUp).
3. Which of these do what the agent does: a recall check, price comps, buyer reply drafts, removing photo location data.

The goal is to find what nobody does for free, and what a casual Seller already pays for. Use the vendors' own pages for features and prices.

## Answer

Findings: [research/03-competitors-and-prices.md](../research/03-competitors-and-prices.md) (also on branch `research/competitors`). Prices came from site-restricted searches because vendor pages were blocked; re-check them before quoting.

- **Paid tools are monthly reseller subscriptions**, $9–50/month (Flyp, SellerAider, Vendoo, Nifty, List Perfectly, Crosslist, OneShop, PrimeLister). Only List Perfectly clearly includes price lookups.
- **Platforms now do the basic job free:** Facebook's AI listing help (March 2026) and Meta's Seller app (July 2026) turn photos into a listing with a suggested price; eBay builds listings from photos and shows sold prices free; OfferUp suggests titles and descriptions.
- **Nobody offers:** a recall check, full buyer-reply drafts with scam screening, photo location-data removal, a floor-and-drop pricing plan on Facebook, or comps from several sources reviewed by a person.
- **Bottom line:** casual Sellers pay nothing for listing tools today. A $5–10 price competes with free tools that are good enough, so the pitch is the safety and judgment layer, not "AI writes your listing".
