Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 02, 03, 06

## Question

Pick what a pilot Seller pays for and how: per Item, a pack of Items, or monthly; the price; what's included; the turnaround time; and what happens if the Item doesn't sell. Draw on the competitor research, the Claude cost per Item, the sample listing pack, and whatever the own-Items log shows so far.
