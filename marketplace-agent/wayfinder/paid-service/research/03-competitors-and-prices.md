# Research: What do similar listing tools offer, and what do they charge?

Ticket: [`issues/03-competitors-and-prices.md`](../issues/03-competitors-and-prices.md)
Checked: **2026-11-11** (all prices and features below are as of this date unless noted)

## How this was researched, and its limits

- **Sources:** each search was restricted to the vendor's or platform's own domain (for example `vendoo.co`, `about.fb.com`, `ebay.com`, `help.offerup.com`), so every claim below traces to a first-party page. Third-party pages are used only where noted, and only for things no first party covers.
- **Limit: pages were not opened directly.** This session's network blocks direct fetches of vendor sites (the proxy refused `www.vendoo.co`, `listperfectly.com`, `crosslist.com` and `joinflyp.com`). So the facts come from a search index of those first-party pages, not from loading each page live. Prices can lag a few weeks. **Re-check any price on the vendor's page before quoting it to a Seller.**
- **Bias warning:** most crosslister feature pages are marketing. Where they compare themselves to rivals, only their claims about *their own* product are used here.
- Items marked **⚠ unverified** could not be confirmed from a first-party source.

Notes:
- **Free trials** are the norm: Flyp gives 100 days, SellerAider 14 days, PrimeLister and OneShop 7 days. Crosslist instead offers a 3-day money-back guarantee for 20 or fewer listings.
- **"Sell it for me" exists too:** Flyp's Pro Sellers program has you ship items to a pro who lists and sells them, with a 90-day unsold policy ([what's next](https://www.joinflyp.com/hire-what-next), [unsold policy](https://www.joinflyp.com/pro-unsold-policy)). ⚠ The commission rate was not on any page found. This is the done-for-you model (A), which is out of scope, but a Seller may compare against it.
- **Standalone AI listing apps** exist, for example "Marketplace Listing AI Copy" on Google Play, which turns a photo into a title, price and description ([Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.chaupham1092.marketplacelistingai)). ⚠ Its price and quality were not verified.
- **General chatbots are the real free option.** ChatGPT's free tier allows "3 file uploads per day", and ChatGPT Go costs $8/mo in the US ([Free tier FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9275245-chatgpt-free-tier-faq), [Go launch](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-go/), [pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/)). A casual Seller can already get a photo-to-description draft for nothing.

Evidence for the less obvious cells:
- **Recall:** Meta lists "Recalled Products" as prohibited ([Commerce Policies](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce)). Its seller agreement makes the seller "solely responsible… for complying with any recall" ([Commerce Product Seller Agreement](https://www.facebook.com/legal/commerce_product_merchant_agreement)). eBay bars recalled items and tells sellers to check recall notices before listing ([eBay product safety policy](https://www.ebay.com/help/policies/prohibited-restricted-items/product-safety-policy?id=4300)). The CPSC says selling a recalled product violates federal law ([eBay's CPSC page](https://www.ebay.com/sellercenter/resources/consumer-product-safety-commission-regulations)). So both platforms enforce after the fact and leave the check to the seller. The only recall checkers found are developer tools: Apify actors "from $3.00 / 1,000 recall checks" ([Apify](https://apify.com/robot-supply-store/recall-check-for-resellers)) and an open-source project ([RecallDesk](https://github.com/SoumyaEXE/recalldesk)). None is a casual-seller product, and none is built into a listing flow.
- **EXIF/GPS:** no vendor in the table advertises removing location data. Meta's help pages found don't say whether Facebook strips photo metadata on upload ([location settings](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/dialog/manage-your-location-information-settings/)). ⚠ **Unverified either way.** It is testable: upload a geotagged photo to a draft listing, then download it and inspect the file.
- **Reply drafts:** Meta's auto-reply is scoped to "when buyers ask about item availability" ([Mar 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/facebook-marketplace-new-meta-ai-tools-make-selling-faster-and-easier/)). No crosslister found drafts general buyer replies. Vendoo and Nifty automate *offers*, not conversations.

## Bottom line for the pilot

**What nobody does for free (or at all, found here):**
1. **A recall check before listing.** The platforms ban recalled items but leave the check to the Seller. No listing tool or platform AI does it.
2. **Full buyer-reply drafts with scam screening.** Meta only auto-answers "is this available?". Nothing drafts haggling, pickup or scam-suspect replies from the Item's fact sheet.
3. **Location-data safety for photos.** Nobody advertises it, and Meta doesn't document what it strips (⚠ test it).
4. **A floor price and drop plan on Facebook.** Mercari has Smart Pricing, but Facebook has no equivalent.
5. **Cross-source comps in one place** (local asking + eBay + retail), plus a human sanity check before posting.

**What's now free, so it can't be the selling point:**
- Photo → title, description, category and a local price suggestion is now built into Facebook (Mar 2026), Meta's Seller app (Jul 2026), eBay, OfferUp, and partly Mercari.
- eBay sold-price comps are free to any eBay seller via Product Research. This also bears on the map's "better price comparisons" open item, but it needs an eBay sign-in, which is where the agent failed.

**What a casual Seller already pays for:**
- **Usually nothing for tools.** Every paid listing tool is a monthly reseller subscription: Flyp $9, SellerAider $12.99, Vendoo $14.99, then $25–$60+. They are sold on crosslisting volume, not on one person clearing a garage.
- What casual Sellers do pay is **platform fees** (Facebook 10% on shipped sales, eBay fees) and sometimes a **general AI chatbot** (ChatGPT Go $8, Plus ~$20).
- So **$5–10 one-off, per batch or per item, sits in an empty slot.** It's cheaper than any reseller subscription, but it competes with "free and good enough" from Meta.
- The pitch should therefore be the **safety and judgment layer**: recall check, scam-aware reply pack, location stripping, a floor price and drop plan, and multi-source comps checked by a person. "AI writes your listing" is free now.
